// Research & Publications

IEEE Publication

Third IEEE International Conference (RTEICT) · 2020
A Novel Approach to Detect Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia
Proposed and implemented an automated image processing pipeline in MATLAB for early-stage detection of Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL) using digital blood smear images from the ALL-IDB dataset (University of Milano, Italy). The system applied colour-based thresholding for white blood cell detection, L*a*b colour space segmentation to isolate nuclei and cytoplasm, and Watershed Transform algorithms to separate adjoining cells. Lymphoblasts were identified by computing nucleus roundness and area thresholds — flagging ALL-positive cases when two or more lymphoblasts were detected.
